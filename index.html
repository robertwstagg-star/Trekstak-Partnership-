FAQ

Is the $150 guaranteed?

It applies to founding creators who are accepted and complete the agreed initial collaboration, as specified in their partnership terms — not to every applicant.

How does the 25% commission work?

You get a unique referral link. Qualifying subscriptions attributed to that link earn commission under the Creator Partner Program terms.

Can I earn commission after my first post?

Yes. Your link can continue generating qualifying commissions after the initial collaboration, subject to the program terms.

Do I need a large following?

No. Audience relevance and engagement matter more than follower count.

What platforms can I use?

YouTube, Instagram, TikTok, blogs, and other channels may be accepted depending on the partnership.

Can this become more than one collaboration?

That’s the aim. Future campaigns aren’t guaranteed — they depend on fit and performance.
