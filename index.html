FAQ

Who receives the starting collaboration payment?

Founders accepted into the program and who complete their initial collaboration get the payment set in their partnership terms.

How does the promo code work?

You get a unique code; your audience gets 20% off their first year. Every qualifying annual subscription using your code is tracked to you.

How does commission work?

Qualifying annual subscriptions using your code earn commission as outlined in the Creator Partner Program terms.

Do I have to keep creating sponsored content?

No. The initial collaboration is the beginning. After that, you can share TrekStak whenever it fits your content and audience.

When do I get paid?

Your initial payment comes after you complete and get approval on your agreed content. It isn’t tied to Apple’s App Store payment cycle.

Commission appears in your dashboard with each qualifying subscription. Cash payouts follow Apple’s payment schedule after TrekStak receives proceeds.

View the Commission Payment Schedule →

How does the $1,000 travel reward work?

Reach 500 qualifying annual subscribers and earn a $1,000 airline gift card, on top of your regular commission.

What’s expected for @trekstak.app?

Each month: two features of your content on the official TrekStak Instagram, and two genuine shout-outs to @trekstak.app from your own account. It’s how we grow the home base together — not a requirement to turn every post into an ad.

Is there a minimum following?

We generally look for Instagram creators with 5,000+ followers, but we also care about fit, engagement, creativity, and content quality.

Do I need an iPhone?

Yes. TrekStak is currently available only on iPhone, so you’ll need one to join.

What platforms can I use?

Instagram is our core platform, but TikTok and YouTube are welcome if they fit your style and audience.

Organic, not forced

We don’t expect your feed to become a stream of TrekStak ads. Just let the app show up naturally when it’s part of your planning or travel process — no need to mention the brand every time. Often, a little curiosity, a few comments, and your bio link do more than any hard sell.

  • Planning Creator You plan trips in public. Content isn’t an ad — it’s finding a natural way to work TrekStak in, without always naming the brand.
    • Create “plan with me,” day stacks, and itinerary content you’d naturally want to share.
    • Show the app on screen as you talk through your trip; mentioning TrekStak by name every time is optional.
    • Spark curiosity — when followers ask what app you’re using, share your link or code in your reply.
    • Keep your promo link or code in your bio and add a “how I plan” highlight to your profile.
    • A soft call-to-action in your caption is enough. Always disclose when a post is paid or includes affiliate links.
    • The founding offer remains the same — this is your chance to welcome fellow planners to TrekStak.
  • Destination Specialist You go deep on one or two places. Guides and tips are your thing; TrekStak is how you organize the trip for your audience.
  • Lifestyle Creator Travel life, day-in-the-life, tips. You keep TrekStak light in content and strong in bio, Stories, and natural mentions.
  • Long-form / YouTube Longer videos — vlogs, planning walkthroughs, itineraries. About one TrekStak-related planning video a month that still feels like your normal content, with your code in the description.
