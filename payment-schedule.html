Commission Payment Schedule

Commission payments follow Apple’s App Store remittance cycle. Once Apple pays TrekStak for a period, eligible creator commissions are paid according to our terms.

Two Timelines

Initial Collaboration Payment
Paid after you complete and get approval on your content. Not tied to Apple’s schedule.

Commission
Tracked in your dashboard as subscriptions convert. Cash payouts follow once Apple remits proceeds to TrekStak.

Example
If referrals subscribe during Apple’s August period (August 2–29, 2026), Apple typically pays TrekStak on October 1, 2026. Eligible commission is then paid after proceeds are received.

What to Expect

  • Dashboard earnings may show up before cash hits your account.
  • Commission payouts usually continue monthly after proceeds are received.
  • Refunds or cancellations may adjust pending commission before payout.
  • Apple’s bank posting may vary by region/holidays.
  • We’ll update this page with future schedule details.
